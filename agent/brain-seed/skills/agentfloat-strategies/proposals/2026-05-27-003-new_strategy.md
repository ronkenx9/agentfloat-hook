# Momentum Strategy

## Reasoning
The current strategies have limitations, and introducing a Momentum Strategy can potentially improve yield. The recent proposal for a Momentum Strategy is still pending, and there is evidence from the history that new strategies can be effective, such as the promotion of Mock Yield Strategy on 2026-05-26. The scoring rules have been effective in promoting better strategies, with a minDeltaBps of 10 and minEpochsConsecutive of 5, as seen in the agentfloat-scoring.md file.

## Proposed change
Implement a Momentum Strategy that buys assets with increasing prices and sells assets with decreasing prices, with a momentum indicator such as the Relative Strength Index (RSI) or Moving Average Convergence Divergence (MACD).

## Expected outcome
The Momentum Strategy is expected to perform well in trending markets, potentially increasing the overall yield of the AgentFloat system.

## Evidence
agentfloat-history.md, agentfloat-scoring.md, and the recent proposal for a Momentum Strategy

## Risks
The Momentum Strategy may not perform well in ranging or volatile markets, and its implementation should be carefully evaluated to minimize potential risks.
